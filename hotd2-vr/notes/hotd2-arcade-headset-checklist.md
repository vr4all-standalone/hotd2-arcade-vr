# HOTD2 Arcade VR headset checklist

Physical headset testing has not been performed for this adaptation.

- Install the APK beside the other ports; confirm the HOTD2 identity and setup layout.
- Test missing/corrupt cartridge and BIOS messages, All files access and launch from Download.
- Verify first-run complete pistol preparation, B/Y skip, cache reuse and corrupt-cache recovery.
- Confirm comfortable scale, stereo depth and the widened view in early and later chapters.
- Check aiming at the centre, edges and corners; reload by B/Y, shoot-away and point-away.
- Empty a six-round magazine; verify six accepted-shot kicks, empty clicks and no reload kick.
- Start P1, join P2, transfer the solo gun and reverse both hands in settings.
- Hold each trigger through a hand swap; confirm no shot until release and a fresh pull.
- Confirm empty hands during story scenes and restored guns when shooting resumes.
- Check pause persistence, recenter, tracking/focus loss and battery/controller reconnects.
- Measure sustained frame rate and comfort on each supported headset model.
- Complete later chapters, bosses, continue screens, both players leaving and endings.
