## The House of the Dead 2 v1.0.12

- New Gun, World, System and In-game settings tabs and one menu pointer.
- Choose Green or Red blood in In-game. Green is the default and changes apply on the next start.
- Crosshair defaults to 22% minimum, 40% maximum and 6.5 m shrink distance. The gameplay HUD no longer intercepts aim.
- Laser Off, Normal, 5x and 10x. Right stick click toggles the laser and Crosshair.
- Either controller can Start solo play. The free hand can request P2.
- First-time VR pistol setup uses your own game files. Wider view adds Fade.
- Quest library label is The House of the Dead 2 - VR4ALL.
